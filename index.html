Everything you need to know about legal stay stamps, changing employers, and official Voivodeship fees.

As soon as your complete application is submitted before your current visa or visa-free period expires, the Voivodeship Office places an official stamp (stempel w paszporcie) in your passport. Under Article 108 of the Polish Act on Foreigners, your stay in Poland remains completely legal until a final decision is issued—even if your original visa expires months earlier.
A Work Permit (Zezwolenie na Pracę Typ A) authorizes you to work for a specific Polish employer, and is typically used to obtain a National D-Visa at a Polish Embassy abroad or to start work while holding another valid visa. A Karta Pobytu Czasowego (Single Permit) combines both your right to reside in Poland and your right to work into a single biometric residence card valid for up to 3 years, allowing you to travel across the Schengen Zone without needing new visas.
Official Polish government fees are paid directly to the local municipal/Voivodeship tax account: 440 PLN stamp duty for a Temporary Residence & Work Permit (or EU Blue Card), 340 PLN for Family Reunification or Study/Business residence, plus 100 PLN for printing the biometric plastic Karta Pobytu card once approved. If we represent you via Power of Attorney (Pełnomocnictwo), the statutory tax duty is 17 PLN.
